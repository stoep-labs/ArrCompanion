ArrCompanion — Help

Sonarr & Radarr for Home Screen Companion's missing titles
What does this plugin do?
Home Screen Companion (4.5 or later) lists the titles on your External and AI lists that are not in your library, when its Settings › Show missing titles is on. ArrCompanion shows that list with each title's state in Sonarr or Radarr, and adds the ones you pick.
playlist_remove Missing

Movies and shows are listed as Home Screen Companion has them: poster, title, year, IMDb id and the lists each one is on. A title on several lists is shown once. Search by title or IMDb id, show one source, sort by rank, title, year or number of sources, pick All / Movies / Shows, and switch between the list and posters. The list is made during Home Screen Companion's sync; Refresh reads it again. Posters come from Emby's own metadata search (e.g. TheMovieDb).

Rank — a title's place on its list, once Home Screen Companion sends it.

With Sonarr or Radarr saved, a title that is already there shows ✓ Radarr / ✓ Sonarr (point at it for monitored, downloaded and episodes). A title that is not there gets + Radarr / + Sonarr: pick the root folder and quality profile (Radarr also minimum availability and Monitored) and it is added, monitored, with a search for it. Shows are matched and added by their TVDb id (else IMDb id), movies by their IMDb id (else TMDb id).

settings Settings

Sonarr & Radarr — each server's URL and API key (in Sonarr / Radarr under Settings › General). Test Connection shows the version. A saved key is only sent to its saved URL. The plugin talks to Sonarr and Radarr itself, so they need not be reachable from the browser.
